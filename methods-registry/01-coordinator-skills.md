# Coordinator Skills

CANVAS/Popovic concepts are not atomic methods. They are coordinator skills: lenses, planners, and evaluators that select, sequence, compare, and adapt atomic methods.

Coordinator artifact IDs use `<3digit-id>-<slug>`. The three-digit ID itself signals that the artifact is a coordinator. The first digit is the primary domain group that the coordinator mostly operates over; cross-domain coordinators are assigned to their main effect group.
