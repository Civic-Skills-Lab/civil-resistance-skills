# March Intake Questionnaire

Use this reference when the user has not supplied enough context to build a March Planning Package. Ask only for missing information.

## Minimum Intake

- **Purpose:** What should the march accomplish in one sentence?
- **Strategic fit:** Why use a moving procession rather than a static rally, vigil, speech, or online action?
- **Primary audience:** Who must see, hear, join, or respond first?
- **Secondary audience:** Press, institutional observers, opponents, online viewers, or later participants?
- **Turnout model:** How many participants are expected, how was that estimate made, and what range is plausible?
- **Constituency and speaker standing:** Who is visibly represented, and who has authority to speak or convene?
- **Route idea:** What start, object of attention, endpoint, and symbolic route logic are proposed?
- **Date and timing:** What date, time of day, duration, weather, transit, or competing events matter?
- **Legal posture:** What permit, public-forum, street-use, sound, and recording questions are known?
- **Accessibility needs:** Mobility, language, sensory, childcare, bathroom, seating, water, transit, and rest needs.
- **Constraints:** Budget, volunteers, partner commitments, venue rules, safety concerns, or organizational boundaries.
- **Counter-mobilization:** What hostile group, counter-frame, policing posture, or community concern is likely?
- **Endpoint:** What happens at the end: speech, petition, structured exit, next-event sign-up, vigil, or dispersal?
- **Documentation:** Who can capture stills, video, turnout count, incidents, and press materials?

## Completion Standard

The intake is sufficient when the agent can state the march purpose, audience, turnout assumption, route rationale, legal unknowns, accessibility needs, core roles, likely risks, and endpoint without guessing.
