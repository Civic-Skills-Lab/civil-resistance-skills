Why this exists

Civil-resistance methods are usually published as prose taxonomies. Agents need something more explicit: provenance, risk boundaries, operational availability, references, and completion criteria.

Source basis

The registry crosswalks the Beer/NVI/ICNC tactic universe with Sharp's method taxonomy. Primary sources identify the method; secondary training sources inform low-risk preparation patterns.

Risk-gated transformation

Low-risk methods can become operational skills with planning, research, review, and debrief support. High-risk methods remain descriptive for education, analysis, and simulation until explicitly reviewed.

For whom

Researchers, civic educators, agent builders, simulation designers, movement-support analysts, and practitioners who need structured learning rather than slogans.

Simulation path

The skills are shaped as structured artifacts: phases, artifacts, guards, risk flags, and readiness verdicts. That makes them suitable for future sandbox and game agents.
