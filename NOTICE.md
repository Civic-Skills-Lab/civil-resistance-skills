# Notice and Source Attribution

Civil Resistance Skills is a public research, education, simulation, and
risk-aware preparation project. It organizes civil-resistance method records
into AI-agent skill artifacts, but it is not a campaign organizer and does not
grant rights in third-party source works.

## Rights and Relicensing Caveat

Third-party source materials remain under their original rights, licenses, and
terms. This repository cites, crosswalks, indexes, and paraphrases source
material where appropriate. It does not copy source prose wholesale and does not
relicense third-party works.

In particular, no license grant is made here over source-owned method names,
method numbers, taxonomy structures, database entries, book text, training
materials, diagrams, exercises, case descriptions, or website content beyond
rights held by this project or uses otherwise permitted by law.

## Project Licensing

- Code and packaging infrastructure: Apache-2.0.
- Original project prose, skill text, templates, references, and website copy:
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- Source-derived registry facts and crosswalk metadata: attributed reference
  metadata; underlying source rights remain with their respective owners.

## No Endorsement

References to source authors, publishers, institutions, and organizations are
for attribution, provenance, and research context only. They do not imply
endorsement of this repository, its AI-agent packaging, or any generated skill.

## Source Discipline

Future contributions should:

- cite sources used for method identity, evidence, or preparation guidance;
- paraphrase training and handbook material rather than copying prose;
- avoid reproducing exercises, diagrams, templates, or source text from
  copyrighted manuals unless the license clearly allows it;
- keep high-risk methods descriptive unless explicitly reviewed;
- preserve source caveats when adding generated skills, adapters, or website
  material.
