## Message Box

- **Communication goal:** What should this appearance change?
- **Audience starting point:** What does the audience already think or feel?
- **Vision:** What value, future, or public good is the speech pointing toward?
- **Barrier:** What problem, decision, pattern, or failure blocks that vision?
- **Action:** What lawful, nonviolent next step should listeners take?
- **Counter-frame:** What opposing story or misunderstanding must the speech anticipate?
- **Response frame:** What accurate, non-inflammatory frame should replace or answer it?

## Proof and Examples

- **Proof point 1:** Verified fact, public record, lived example, or named commitment.
- **Proof point 2:** Verified fact, public record, lived example, or named commitment.
- **Proof point 3:** Optional; include only if it strengthens clarity.
- **Evidence to avoid:** Unverified allegation, private detail, inflammatory phrasing, or weak number.

- **Tone:**

- **Delivery format:** notes, full script, memorized outline, press statement, livestream, or public comment.
- **Drafting constraints:** required language, words to avoid, facts that must be sourced, speaker comfort boundaries, and any game/runtime limits.
- **Best outline format:** public narrative, stump-speech, short statement, or issue briefing.
- **Media line:** One short, accurate sentence that can survive quotation or social clipping without misleading context.
- **Transition needs:** Where the speech must move from story to evidence, evidence to claim, and claim to action.
- **Read-aloud test:** What should be cut if the draft sounds written rather than spoken?
- **Endpoint:** Petition, meeting request, pledge, turnout ask, public comment, donation, volunteer action, or another lawful action.
