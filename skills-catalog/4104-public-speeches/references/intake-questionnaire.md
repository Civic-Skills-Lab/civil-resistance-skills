# Public Speech Intake Questionnaire

Use this reference when the user has not yet supplied enough context to prepare a speech brief. Ask only for missing information. For sensitive situations, let the user answer at a level of detail that is safe to share.

## Minimum Intake

- **Goal:** What should this speech accomplish in one sentence?
- **Listener action:** What should the audience know, feel, and do afterward?
- **Primary audience:** Who must understand or move first: supporters, undecided listeners, institutional actors, press, or a local community?
- **Secondary audience:** Who may see clips, quotes, photos, or reports after the event?
- **Speaker standing:** Why is this speaker a legitimate voice for this claim: lived experience, role, mandate, expertise, constituency relationship, or public accountability?
- **Issue context:** What facts, dates, institutions, decisions, harms, promises, or public records matter?
- **Venue and channel:** Where and when will the speech be delivered, and will it be live, recorded, streamed, quoted, or summarized?
- **Length and timing:** How much time is available, and is a short backup version needed?
- **Delivery format:** Will the speaker use notes, a written script, a memorized outline, a lectern, microphone, camera, interpreter, or remote platform?
- **Speaker comfort:** What topics, stories, language, or delivery choices feel credible and safe for the speaker?
- **Language and register:** What language, dialect, formality level, or accessibility needs should shape the wording?
- **Constraints:** What legal, safety, reputational, organizational, or venue constraints are already known?
- **Counter-frame:** What misunderstanding, criticism, opponent frame, or hostile question is most likely?
- **Resources:** Who can document, fact-check, handle media, support rehearsal, and capture debrief notes?

## Public Narrative Prompts

Use these prompts when the speech needs moral energy, shared identity, or a call to action:

- **Self:** What lived experience, choice, or responsibility explains why this speaker is speaking now?
- **Us:** What shared value, memory, identity, or stake connects the speaker to the audience?
- **Now:** What urgent choice, deadline, contradiction, or opportunity makes action timely?
- **Hope:** What credible path lets listeners believe their action matters?
- **Action:** What is the smallest clear next step listeners can take without confusion?

## Audience Movement Prompts

- Where is the primary audience now: unaware, skeptical, sympathetic, ready, fatigued, fearful, or opposed?
- What must change first: attention, understanding, trust, emotion, social permission, or willingness to act?
- What tone will help that movement: calm, solemn, hopeful, urgent, accountable, grateful, or defiant within lawful nonviolent bounds?
- What would make this audience tune out, feel misrepresented, or doubt the speaker?

## Completion Standard

The intake is sufficient when the agent can state the speech goal, audience model, speaker rationale, core issue, delivery constraints, likely counter-frame, and next action without guessing.
