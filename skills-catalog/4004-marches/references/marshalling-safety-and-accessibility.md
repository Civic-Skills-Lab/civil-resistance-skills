# Marshalling, Safety, and Accessibility

Use this reference to plan role clarity and participant support. It is a planning checklist, not a substitute for local legal, medical, accessibility, or safety expertise.

## Role Boundaries

- **Marshals / stewards:** help with pace, route clarity, participant information, and de-escalation posture; they are not police.
- **Route lead / tail:** keep the procession legible and help identify gaps or stragglers.
- **Segment contacts:** support large columns, partner blocs, accessibility groups, or language groups.
- **Medical support:** handles basic care path and escalation, if available.
- **Legal observers:** observe and document; they do not manage the crowd or speak for organizers.
- **Accessibility lead:** checks mobility, language, sensory, rest, bathroom, and transit needs.
- **Endpoint owner:** makes the close coherent and prevents drift into unmanaged dispersal.

## Marshal / Steward Brief

Record:

- visible identifiers that do not resemble police, security, or emergency uniforms
- communication channel and backup if phones fail
- who can change pace, pause, shorten, reroute, or end the march
- how to communicate route information to participants
- how to respond to confusion without escalating
- who contacts medical, legal, media, accessibility, or endpoint leads

## Accessibility and Participant Support

- accessible route options and rest points
- wheelchair / mobility device constraints
- language interpretation or plain-language instructions
- sensory needs around sound, crowding, lights, and chants
- water, toilets, shade, warming, or weather plan
- buddy or reconnect plan for lost participants
- clear exit instructions for people who need to leave early

## Safety and De-Escalation Posture

Plan for reducing confusion and avoiding unnecessary confrontation:

- keep instructions simple and repeated
- separate observer, marshal, and spokesperson roles
- keep participant-facing communication factual
- identify flashpoints and alternative paths before the event
- document incidents without directing unsafe confrontation

## Readiness Check

The staffing plan is coherent when role leads, communication path, route authority, accessibility support, and medical/legal escalation path are named or explicitly marked unavailable.
