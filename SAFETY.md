# Safety and Use Policy

Civil Resistance Skills is research, education, simulation, and risk-aware
preparation infrastructure. It is not a campaign organizer, operational command
system, or tool for coordinating real-world unlawful activity.

## Allowed Project Use

This repository is intended for:

- studying civil-resistance method taxonomies and historical patterns;
- building AI-agent skills for research, civic education, and simulation;
- preparing low-risk public communication artifacts with legal, narrative,
  reputational, and source-review guardrails;
- modeling future sandbox/game agents that can reason about civic tactics
  without executing real-world actions.

## Disallowed Use

Do not use this repository or its skills to request, produce, or coordinate:

- violence, threats, intimidation, or incitement;
- property damage, sabotage, weapons use, or physically dangerous conduct;
- doxxing, harassment, targeted abuse, impersonation, or privacy invasion;
- malware, DDoS, unauthorized access, credential theft, or technical abuse;
- evasion of law, lawful safety directions, venue rules, platform rules, or
  permit conditions;
- deceptive attribution, fabricated evidence, fake endorsements, or defamatory
  claims;
- step-by-step execution guidance for illegal, coercive, or dangerous activity.

## Risk-Gated Skill Policy

Low-risk methods may include planning, research, review, rehearsal, and debrief
support. High-risk methods remain descriptive and are held for education,
analysis, and simulation unless explicitly reviewed.

Operational skills must preserve source discipline: distinguish verified facts,
user claims, and assumptions; do not invent citations; and surface unresolved
legal, safety, privacy, attribution, and reputational risks.

## Legal and Professional Advice

Nothing in this repository is legal, security, medical, or professional advice.
Users are responsible for local law, platform rules, venue rules, and safety
requirements in their jurisdiction.
